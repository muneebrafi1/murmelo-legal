Privacy Policy

Murmelo — AI voice notes · Effective 16 July 2026

Murmelo turns your voice into notes. To do that, it needs to handle your recordings with care. This page explains, in plain language, exactly what we collect and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Your recordings — the audio you record or upload, stored encrypted in our cloud (Supabase, hosted on AWS in Mumbai) so your notes sync and survive a lost phone.
  • Transcripts and summaries — the text our AI produces from your audio, stored the same way.
  • An anonymous account — Murmelo creates a random account identifier on first launch. We do not require or collect your name, email, or phone number.
  • Basic usage counters — for example, how many free notes you have used.

Who processes your audio

To transcribe and summarize, your audio and transcripts are sent to two processors: AssemblyAI (speech-to-text) and OpenAI (summaries and note chat). They process your data to provide the service and do not use it to train their models under the API terms we use. Your notes are never sold, and we show no ads.

Who can see your notes

Only you. Every note is protected by row-level security tied to your account. Murmelo staff (that's one person) does not read your notes. Notes leave the app only when you tap Share.

Deleting your data

Delete any note from the note's menu — the audio file, transcript, and summary are removed from our servers. To erase everything, use Settings → Delete my data inside the app. Both are immediate and permanent.

Payments

Subscriptions are handled by Apple through your App Store account. We never see your card details.

Voice data consent

By recording with Murmelo you consent to your voice being processed as described above. Please make sure you have the agreement of other people you record — laws about recording conversations vary by country.
