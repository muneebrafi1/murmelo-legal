Common questions

How do I cancel?

In Murmelo: Settings → Manage or cancel. Or on your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Murmelo. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Cancelling during the free trial means you are not charged.

When does the free trial end?

7 days after you start it, on the yearly plan. With notifications on, Murmelo reminds you 2 days before.

I got a new phone. How do I get my Pro back?

On the subscription screen, tap Restore. Murmelo checks your Apple Account.

How do I keep my notes when I change phones?

Before you switch: Settings → Back up with Apple. On the new phone, open Settings → Back up with Apple with the same Apple Account and your notes come back.

A recording hasn't turned into notes.

Recordings are saved on your phone first. Open Murmelo with a connection and it uploads by itself; if a lecture says it needs attention, tap Try again. Still stuck? Email us and we'll look.

Can I record with the screen locked?

Yes. Start recording, then lock your phone. Murmelo keeps recording until you tap Stop & save.

How do I delete my data?

Delete one lecture from its menu (it stays in Recently deleted for 30 days), or delete everything with Settings → Delete account.

Is it OK to record my lectures?

Follow your university's rules and the law where you are, and ask your lecturer when that's required.
